info About Microswab

Microswab is a community-driven learning platform where people can learn practical skills, share knowledge, connect with other learners, and grow together.

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced professional, Microswab gives you the opportunity to improve your skills while helping others improve theirs.

Our mission is to make quality skill learning simple, interactive, and accessible for everyone.

lightbulb Why Microswab Was Created

Microswab was created because learning valuable skills should not be limited by expensive courses or complicated platforms.

Our goal is to build a community where learning feels practical, social, and motivating.

  • Learn practical skills.
  • Join learning communities.
  • Share stories and progress.
  • Receive feedback.
  • Complete daily challenges.
  • Support and encourage one another.

We believe learning together helps people grow faster than learning alone.

security Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

Microswab may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo
  • Skills you want to learn or teach
  • Posts, stories, comments, messages, and other content you choose to share.

We use this information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide Microswab features.
  • Connect learners and teachers.
  • Improve the platform.
  • Keep the community safe.

We do not intentionally sell your personal information to third parties.

groups Community Guidelines

To keep Microswab safe and welcoming, every user is expected to:

  • Treat everyone with respect.
  • Be honest and helpful.
  • Share original or properly credited work.
  • Give constructive feedback.
  • Avoid spam and misleading content.
  • Avoid harassment, bullying, hate speech, or discrimination.
  • Avoid posting illegal, harmful, or inappropriate content.
  • Respect other people's privacy.

Users who repeatedly violate these guidelines may have their content removed or their accounts suspended.

gavel Terms of Use

By using Microswab, you agree to:

  • Use the platform responsibly.
  • Follow all community guidelines.
  • Be responsible for the content you publish.
  • Respect the rights of other users.
  • Understand that Microswab may update features, policies, or services over time.
  • Accept that accounts violating the rules may be restricted or removed to protect the community.
